## Experimental adapters

Base USDC and native FLR payment adapters remain experimental. Do not enable them unless the intended deployment advertises the exact tuple and your own end-to-end validation covers payment, delivery, receipt, bundle and anchor.

## Local development

Mock mode performs no onchain verification. Local addresses, mock tokens and Coston2 examples are development fixtures and must not be presented as the hosted payment path.
