Claims and limitations

Safe claims

ProofRails can claim that it:

  • records onchain payment references
  • generates ISO 20022-style artifacts
  • packages receipt evidence into bundles
  • hashes evidence bundles
  • anchors hashes onchain when configured
  • verifies evidence integrity and anchor consistency

Claims to avoid

Do not claim that ProofRails:

  • moves funds
  • provides custody
  • guarantees legal settlement finality
  • performs KYC or sanctions screening by default
  • certifies ISO 20022 compliance
  • proves invoice validity
  • proves sender intent
  • guarantees bank acceptance
