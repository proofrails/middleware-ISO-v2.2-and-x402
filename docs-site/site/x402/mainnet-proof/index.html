Mainnet proof walkthrough

The linked transactions are on Flare mainnet and used real USD₮0. This page only inspects existing public evidence and does not submit a payment.

This walkthrough covers the standard-client run completed on 29 July 2026 with unmodified x402 2.19.0 packages.

Open the Flare settlement transaction.

2. Inspect the protected result

The paid endpoint returned:

{"rate":"0.00628055","source":"coingecko"}

The SHA-256 of those exact response bytes is:

0xc73f1a900c736d04ec5253a324241fbb5d3d5fbdcd39d656d8a77c007c2fdca1

That value matches the live receipt and delivery.json inside the signed evidence bundle.

3. Inspect the receipt and bundle

Open receipt dec5a106-3216-4543-bfb6-e669dc83f4d7.

Expected status: anchored.

Expected current bundle hash:

0xe700ed53d6aafad638d8c9b7cb55dc44862a2eee422f3903cdc38c561a3bd4c1

Independent checks of the served ZIP confirmed:

  • ZIP integrity;
  • the ZIP SHA-256 matches the receipt bundle hash;
  • the Ed25519 signature validates over manifest.json;
  • every manifest file matches its declared hash and size;
  • the served pain.001, pain.002 and camt.054 files parse and match their API-declared hashes;
  • the result resource hash appears in the receipt and signed bundle.

These are ISO 20022-style artifacts. Their presence does not establish certification or compliance with a specific institution's market practice.

4. Inspect the anchor

Open the Flare EvidenceAnchor transaction.

The transaction called EvidenceAnchor at:

0x235f83a74fc9D759D648eC533d2c06712F3Ca5EA

Its EvidenceAnchored event committed:

0xe700ed53d6aafad638d8c9b7cb55dc44862a2eee422f3903cdc38c561a3bd4c1

That value equals the hash of the currently served evidence ZIP.

5. Understand the proof boundary

This evidence supports the integrity and timing of the committed receipt bundle and its recorded payment and resource hashes. The anchor does not independently prove that every offchain field is true, that the exchange satisfies a legal obligation, or that the generated XML is certified for a specific ISO 20022 market practice.
